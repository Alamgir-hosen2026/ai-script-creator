Privacy Policy

AI Script Creator Studio · by Alamgir App Studio

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app AI Script Creator Studio (the "App"), published by Alamgir App Studio ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use it. By installing and using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

The App helps you write, improve, plan, edit and export video, podcast and advertising scripts using artificial intelligence (AI).

2. Summary in plain words

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not run our own servers and we do not collect, store or sell your personal data.
  • The text you type into AI tools is sent to third-party AI services (Google Gemini, Groq and Cohere) so they can create your result.
  • Your saved scripts, history, versions, brand voices, settings and backups stay on your phone.
  • The App has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

3. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect or request: your name, email address, phone number, home address, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, videos, microphone or camera data, SMS or call logs, advertising ID, or any government or financial identifiers. The App does not contain advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting services of our own.

4. Information the App handles to work

4.1 Text you enter into AI tools

When you tap Generate (or any similar AI action), the App sends the text you entered (for example your topic, your script, your settings such as language, tone and duration, and, if you turn it on, your saved Brand Voice description and sample) to an AI provider so that it can create the result. The result is sent back to your phone and shown in the App.

This is the only information that leaves your device because of the App's own features, and it is sent only when you ask the App to create something. Requests are sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4.2 Content stored on your phone

The following is stored only in the private storage of the App on your device and is not uploaded to us:

  • Scripts you save or write in the editor, including formatting.
  • Generation history (the results the AI created for you).
  • Version history of your scripts.
  • Brand Voice entries (name, style notes and optional sample text).
  • App settings, such as automatic backup options.
  • A small counter of how many AI tasks you used today, and a saved copy of whether your subscription is active, so the App can apply its daily limits.
  • Backup files (.lkb) created by you or by the automatic backup feature.

4.3 Files you export or back up

If you export a script (TXT, PDF or DOCX), create a backup, or share content, the file is created on your device and saved or shared only where you choose, using Android's file picker or share sheet. After you share a file with another app or person, that app or person's own privacy practices apply.

5. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services. Each one has its own privacy policy and handles information under its own terms.

Important: AI providers may keep and process the text sent to them for a limited time to operate their service, prevent abuse, and, depending on the plan and terms they apply, to improve their products. Free-tier AI services in particular may use submitted content for product improvement. Because of this, please do not enter passwords, financial details, health information, confidential business secrets, or other sensitive personal information into the App.

6. Subscriptions and payments

The App offers an optional monthly subscription (Premium) sold through Google Play Billing. Payment, billing, renewal and cancellation are managed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card number, billing address or other payment details. The App only receives confirmation from Google Play of whether a subscription is active, so it can unlock Premium features.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policies.

7. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: needed to contact the AI services and Google Play and to check whether you are online.
  • The App does not request access to your storage, camera, microphone, location, contacts or phone. Saving and opening files happens through Android's system file picker, where you choose each file or folder yourself.

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this App. Your data is only backed up when you create a backup file yourself or turn on the App's automatic backup, which keeps files on your phone.

8. How we use information

Information handled by the App is used only to:

  • create the AI results you request;
  • save, edit, organise, export and back up your work on your device;
  • apply free daily limits and unlock Premium features for subscribers; and
  • keep the App secure and working properly.

We do not sell your information, rent it, use it for advertising, or build user profiles.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • On your phone: Your content stays until you delete it inside the App (delete a script, clear history, delete a backup or brand voice) or uninstall the App, which removes the App's private data. Backup files you saved or shared outside the App must be deleted by you.
  • With AI providers: We do not control how long Google, Groq or Cohere keep requests. Please read their privacy policies linked above.
  • With us: We hold no copy of your content because the App has no server of ours.

10. Security

Requests to AI services use encrypted HTTPS connections, and your saved content is kept in the App's private storage area, which other apps cannot normally access. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your phone and your exported or backed-up files safe.

11. International data transfers

AI providers may process requests on servers in countries other than your own. By using the AI features you understand that the text you submit may be transferred to and processed in those countries under the providers' terms.

12. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not ask for any personal information. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, please use the App only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an AI provider while using the App, please contact us and the relevant provider.

13. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on our own servers, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California privacy laws), you may have rights over data held by the AI providers or Google. You can use the privacy tools and contact details in their policies to exercise those rights. You can also contact us using the details below and we will help where we can.

14. AI-generated content

  • AI results can be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or unsuitable. Please review everything before you publish or rely on it.
  • You are responsible for how you use the content you create, including making sure it does not infringe the rights of others and follows the rules of the platforms where you publish it.
  • Do not use the App to create content that is illegal, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, deceptive or harmful.
  • You own the scripts you create with the App, to the extent allowed by law and by the terms of the AI providers. Similar results may be produced for other users.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add features or change AI providers. The new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
